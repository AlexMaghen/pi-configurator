pi-fig configures Raspberry Pi devices over Bluetooth LE. A reviewer is unlikely to have a Raspberry Pi running our pi-configurator service, so the app includes a demo device that runs entirely inside the app and needs no hardware or Bluetooth.

To try every screen:
1. Open pi-fig. On the Devices screen, tap the sliders button at the top right (Preferences).
2. Turn on "Show demo device" and go back.
3. Tap "BallsPi" (marked Demo).
4. Enter the password: 1234
5. The device opens on "Device & Network": the Wi-Fi network row opens a list of networks, and below the network settings are the device's info and actions (Activity, Reboot, Disconnect). Switch to "Application Settings" at the top for the device's own settings. Edit any setting and tap Save.

No account or sign-in is needed. The app has no server and makes no internet connections. Bluetooth is used only to find and talk to nearby Raspberry Pi devices.
