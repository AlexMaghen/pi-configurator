pi-fig sets up Raspberry Pi devices over Bluetooth. You don't need a Raspberry Pi to try it: the app has a built-in demo device.

Turn on the demo device
1. Open pi-fig. On the Devices screen, tap the sliders button at the top right (Preferences).
2. Turn on "Show demo device", then go back.
3. "BallsPi" appears in the list, marked Demo. Tap it.
4. Enter the password 1234 and tap Unlock. The app saves the password on this phone, so next time it opens straight away.

Device & Network
5. The device page opens on "Device & Network" (the switch at the top).
6. Tap "Wi-Fi network" to see the networks the device can see. Tap one to join it. Any password works, except on "Neighbors", which shows a wrong-password error.
7. Change the hostname, or pick a time zone from the list (you can search it).
8. Below the network settings are the device's details and actions: Model, Device Data, Activity, Change password, Rename device, Reboot, Forget saved password and Disconnect.
9. Tap "Device Data". A sheet shows the device's hardware, system, network and Bluetooth details. The button at the top right copies them all.
10. Tap "Activity" to see each step the device reported while saving.

Application Settings
11. Tap "Application Settings" at the top. These are the device's own settings.
12. Try a number field (it opens a number pad), the "Home on start" switch, a text field, and "Log level" (a drop-down menu).
13. After a change, Save and Discard buttons appear at the bottom. Tap Save and watch the progress as the device applies it. Or tap Discard to undo your changes.
14. Enter a number outside the allowed range to see the error message.

Leaving
15. The circle-X at the top right of the device page goes back to the device list.

Good to know
- The demo runs entirely inside the app. There is no account, no server and no internet use.
- The demo starts fresh each time the app is reopened. If you changed its password, a "saved password no longer works" note appears; use 1234 again.
- Bluetooth is only used to find real Raspberry Pi devices running the pi-configurator service.

Please report anything confusing, slow or broken.
